1 Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account registration details (name, email, username)
  • Profile information and preferences
  • Communications you send to us (support tickets, feedback)
  • Payment information for premium services (via secure third-party processors)

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information (browser type, OS, device ID)
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used, time spent)
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Log data and error reports

🔒 Important: We do NOT collect, store, or access your encryption keys, plaintext data, passwords used in our cryptographic tools, or any content you encrypt/decrypt.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: Provide, maintain, and improve cryptography tools
  • Account Management: Create and manage your user account
  • Communication: Send service updates, security alerts, respond to inquiries
  • Security: Detect, prevent, and address fraud and security issues
  • Analytics: Understand usage patterns and improve experience
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

ℹ️ We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

3 Cryptographic Data Handling

  • All encryption/decryption operations are performed client-side in your browser
  • We employ AES-256 encryption for any server-side processing
  • Encryption keys generated on our platform are never transmitted to servers
  • Hash computations are local; results are not logged
  • API operations use TLS 1.3 encrypted connections

⚠️ Zero-Knowledge Architecture: We cannot access or recover your encrypted data. If you lose your encryption keys, we cannot help recover your data.

4 Data Storage & Security

  • Data at rest encrypted using AES-256-GCM
  • Data in transit protected via TLS 1.3
  • Passwords hashed using Argon2id with unique salts
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) available for all accounts
  • SOC 2 Type II certified data center infrastructure

5 Cookies & Tracking

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic functionality and security
  • Preference Cookies: Remember settings (theme, language)
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymized usage analytics

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6 Third-Party Services

  • Payment Processors: Stripe, PayPal (own privacy policies)
  • Analytics: Privacy-focused analytics (Plausible / self-hosted Matomo)
  • Infrastructure: Cloud hosting with data protection agreements
  • CDN: Fast and secure content delivery

7 Data Retention

  • Account Data: Deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Usage Logs: Anonymized after 90 days, deleted after 12 months
  • Support Tickets: Retained 24 months after resolution
  • Payment Records: As required by law (typically 7 years)
  • Cryptographic Operations: No retention — processed and discarded

8 Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive data in machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit processing of your data
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw at any time

Contact us at privacy@cryptoshield.com to exercise these rights.

9 Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware, we will delete such information promptly.

10 Changes to This Policy

  • Registered users notified via email
  • Prominent notice displayed on platform
  • Summary of key changes provided
